AgriRAG: Training-Free Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Agricultural Disease Diagnosis with Vision-Language Models

Luiz Carlos Marques Junior, João Renato Ribeiro Manesco, João Paulo Papa; Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR) Workshops, 2026, pp. 9673-9681

Abstract

We present AgriRAG, a training-free, visual-first Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline for agricultural pathology diagnosis, where retrieval is driven entirely by image embeddings without gradient updates. It couples a SigLIP-based image retrieval module with the Qwen3-VL-8B vision-language model, running via 4-bit quantization on a consumer-grade NVIDIA RTX 4070 Ti GPU (12 GB VRAM). Unlike prior approaches that require fine-tuning on domain-specific data, AgriRAG leverages a unified knowledge base of 74,611 embeddings of expert-verified facts fused from two complementary agricultural corpora (AgMMU and MIRAGE), dynamically retrieving the most relevant context at inference time without gradient updates. We evaluate on the AgMMU benchmark with 1,635 samples across six agronomic categories. AgriRAG achieves 83.7 percent MCQ accuracy (95 percent CI: 81.9 percent to 85.5 percent), surpassing all open-source baselines, including InternVL2-26B (68.1 percent) and LLaVA-1.6-34B (61.3 percent), and narrowing the gap to GPT-4o (86.2 percent) to 2.5 percentage points (McNemar p-value below 0.001). An ablation study reveals a 40.2 percentage point gain from zero-shot to RAG-augmented inference, showing that retrieval, not parameter scale, is the primary performance driver for agricultural visual reasoning. These results show that high-quality crop diagnostics are achievable on commodity hardware without cloud APIs or domain-specific fine-tuning. On the MIRAGE generative benchmark (502 queries, 7 categories), AgriRAG further achieves a 46 percent relative improvement over GPT-4.1 in entity identification (0.640 vs. 0.439) and a 15.9 percent improvement in management guidance (W-Sum 0.950 vs. 0.820), establishing a new state of the art for training-free agricultural generative reasoning.
